About this reconstruction +

Reconstructed from 240 source frames. A clean face template was fitted to 26 source views to repair the cheek and temple cavities. Eyes and eyelids were rebuilt, followed by lips, teeth, tongue and a smooth continuous jaw rig. Hair is retained. The shoulder repair seals part of the original mechanical openings. Unobserved views remain approximate.

The mouth and chin share continuous skin weights. Lip correctives, skeletal motion and blinks run live in Three.js. Teeth and tongue are inferred; fine scanned surface marks remain. Synthetic speech uses approximate timing.

The texture includes baked lighting. Studio mode adds light to this appearance; it is not a separated albedo material.
