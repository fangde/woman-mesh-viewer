About this reconstruction +

Reconstructed from 240 source frames. A clean face template was fitted to 26 source views to repair the cheek and temple cavities. Hair is retained. The shoulder repair seals part of the original mechanical openings. Unobserved views remain approximate.

The texture includes baked lighting. Studio mode adds light to this appearance; it is not a separated albedo material.
